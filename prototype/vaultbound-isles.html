Vaultbound Isles

Twelve islands wait beyond the lobby portals. Fight through their stages, beat their bosses, and hunt for Vaulted gear.

CommonRareEpicLegendaryVaulted 0.01% to 0.5%
  • Move with the touch joystick, WASD, or a controller. Drag to look around.
  • Dash up to 3 times in a row. Each dash comes back 1.5 seconds after you use it.
  • Step on a portal to open the island map. Black islands are undiscovered: clear Normal and Hard on an island to reveal the next.
  • Every island has Normal, Hard and Nightmare stages, each made of levels that end in a boss.
  • Level up from fights and spend stat points on Speed, Damage, Defence or Power.
  • Open the Armoury to turn your weapons and armour around in 3D and compare their stats.
