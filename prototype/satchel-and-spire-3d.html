Satchel & Spire

Explore the valley, fight what lives there, and fill your satchel. Every item, weapon, armour piece and class sits on one rarity ladder.

CommonRareEpicLegendaryVaulted
  • Move with the joystick (touch), WASD, or a game controller. Drag the screen to look around.
  • Search crates, ore rocks, shelves and weapon racks with Use. Higher rarity drops shoot up a coloured beam.
  • Roll a class at the Class Altar in camp with a Fortune Die or 10,000 coins.
  • The Vault Spire in the north east is a long climb. Its summit always holds a Vaulted item.
  • Comet Night comes every few minutes. While the sky is purple, Vaulted items can drop anywhere.
